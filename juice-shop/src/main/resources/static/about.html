About us

Small batches, pressed the same day.

Violet Press started with one press, a market stall and a short list of rules about what goes in the bottle.

Simple things, done carefully

We press fruit and vegetables cold, in small batches, and bottle them the same day. No concentrates, no added sugar, no water to stretch a recipe.

Each juice has three or four ingredients. If something doesn't earn its place in the bottle, it comes out.

Our promise: if you can't pronounce it or picture it growing, it isn't in there.

How we work

Pressed fresh

Every bottle is pressed and filled on the day it ships or goes on the shelf.

Chosen with care

We buy seasonal produce from growers we know and change recipes when the harvest does.

Nothing unnecessary

Plain glass, a short ingredient list and packaging that can be reused or recycled.
